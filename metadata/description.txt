Sweet Keyhole is a personal savings garden for one goal at a time. You set aside loose cash, then season it into the trunk until the goal is ripe.

• Plant a goal with a Name and Target
• Wedge cash into the Outer band, then Season it into the trunk
• Drop last undoes the latest add
• Watch progress on Garden and in Commits
• Choose Currency in Settings
• Save a copy of your marks when you want a file
• Erase this garden to clear goals and cash on this device
• Show the introduction again from Settings

Cash stays on this phone unless you save a copy or open Contact.
